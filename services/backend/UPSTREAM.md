## P3 scope

The backend keeps the official administration, authentication, system, RBAC, menu, permission, audit, logging, and health foundations required by P3. The `ruoyi-ai`, `ruoyi-demo`, `ruoyi-gen`, `ruoyi-job`, `ruoyi-workflow`, and `ruoyi-extend` applications/modules are deliberately omitted because they are outside the approved phase boundary.

Local changes are limited to module selection, environment-only runtime configuration, a disabled-by-default bootstrap administrator, the P3 foundation SQL, project naming, and documentation. Product tables, product CRUD, `UnifiedProductOffer`, and platform adapters are not present.
